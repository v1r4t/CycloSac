CycloSac

Core problem: when a cyclone approaches, disaster managers must know what will break, where, when, and who it affects — across power, roads, hospitals, water, and telecom — in time to de-energize, evacuate, reroute, and pre-position. CycloSac fuses satellite tiles, surge/rainfall/wind footprints, asset registers, ward population, and dependency graphs into one decision-grade forecast JSON.

The pipeline

  1. loader — validates 10 inputs, fail-fast (exit 2). No partial runs.
  2. gee_fetcher — Sentinel-1/SRTM/IMERG tile, cache-first (exit 5).
  3. hazard_sim + hazard — Holland wind, SLOSH surge grid, rain index; bands-sum 5%, timeline sorted.
  4. vuln — UTM-32645 polygon buffers (50m ring), 35/25/20/10/10 scoring vs fragility curves.
  5. cascade — BFS from triggers ≥70, depth ≤3, ward-union population.
  6. advisory + Gemini Path1 — SMS ≤280ch, glossary (ଓଡ଼ିଆ), banned-word retry or exit 4.
  7. parametric + counterfactual — wind/surge payout table → mock INR ledger; 0/6/12/24h evacuation slider.
  8. qa + cli — 12 deterministic checks, exact blocker count, exits 0–5, one forecast.json.
